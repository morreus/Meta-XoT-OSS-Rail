# Code of conduct

Meta-XoT-OSS-Rail is for everyone: developers, and people who have never written a line of code. This page says
how we treat each other in its issues and discussions.

## What we ask

- Be kind and patient. Many people here are new to building software.
- Talk about the work, not the person.
- Say clearly what you saw and what you tried, so others can help.
- Accept that people disagree, and say so politely.

## What is not accepted

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Spam, or advertising that has nothing to do with the project.
- Sharing someone else's private information.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close or lock issues, and block anyone who breaks these rules. To
report a problem privately, contact the maintainer on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/smetaxotos).
