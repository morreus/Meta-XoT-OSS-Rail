# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — first public release

The first public release of Meta-XoT-OSS-Rail: the full and the short chain, each step on its own,
the hooks that enforce the process, and the guidance for Flutter, Python and Unity projects. You answer
business questions; when the work needs a development stack your project does not have, Claude asks you once
to choose from up to three options, and sets it up before building. Before every question, Claude explains in the
chat what you are deciding; each question the pipeline asks ends with a reminder to Claude to explain it to you in
the chat first. The README's "Updating" says how to get a new version, and how to be told of one.
